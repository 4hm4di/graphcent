Graphcent is a creative and marketing studio based in Kano, Nigeria, building thoughtful brands, digital experiences and creative work for businesses across Northern Nigeria, and beyond.

OUR STORY

In 2019, from a small home workspace, Graphcent was born. What began as a shared vision among close friends grew into a bold design and media studio, led by a commitment to thoughtful creative work and meaningful digital experiences.

From those beginnings it has grown into a team of eight delivering branding, UI/UX and digital content, helping brands across Northern Nigeria, and beyond, stand out in crowded markets and connect with their audiences.

QUESTIONS

Who do you typically work with?

Ambitious brands, agencies and creatives who want to push boundaries: whether you are launching a product, refreshing an identity or building a full digital experience.

What is your creative process like?

We start by listening closely to understand your goals and challenges. From there we research, develop concepts, storyboard, design and refine until everything is polished. Communication stays open at every stage.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary with scope and complexity, but most projects take between two and eight weeks. We set clear expectations upfront and give regular updates throughout.

Who actually does the work?

Our core team handles creative direction, design, UI/UX, development and media production. For specialised needs we bring in trusted partners such as VFX artists, photographers and sound designers.
