COLORWAVES

The founder came to Graphcent with a business that would span interiors, paint and architectural design, and no name yet. We started with the name. Then we gave it an identity that means something: one wave of paint that becomes a W, in every colour of the rainbow.

The brief

A founder starting a company that does three things most businesses keep apart: interiors, paint and architectural design. Before anything visual, she needed a name that could hold all three, be easy to say and remember, and leave room to grow.

The approach

We explored names around what the business really sells: colour, and the change it brings to a space. ColorWaves stuck: colour in motion. Everything else grew out of the name. The wave became a W, the W became a rainbow of paint, and the rainbow became a palette, a voice and a family of three sub-brands.
