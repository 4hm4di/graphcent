AMEA

AMEA’s old website was down and needed a revamp. Graphcent built the new amea.ng end to end: design, frontend development and the services behind it, from the enquiry forms to email.

The brief

AMEA, an indigenous Nigerian mining and agro-export company based in Kano, came to Graphcent with their old website down and in need of a revamp. They needed a site that presents the company with confidence and turns interest from partners, buyers and job seekers into real enquiries.

The approach

We took it all the way, designed straight in code: every page, every interaction, and the services that keep it running. Enquiries land in a Google Sheet and in the AMEA inbox, analytics only runs with the visitor’s consent, and the site is served on AMEA’s own domain.

Scope

  1. 01Design & frontend21 pages: home, what we do, mining, agriculture, global markets, assets and licences, sustainability, careers, contact, six explainers and the legal pages.
  2. 02Enquiry forms & emailFive enquiry forms post to a Google Apps Script web app: each enquiry becomes a row in a Google Sheet and an email to info@amea.ng. A hidden spam trap stops bots, and a failed send says so; it never fakes success.
  3. 03Interactive licence mapA 3D map of Nigeria with AMEA’s licence areas, and licence records you can filter by type and mineral.
  4. 04Privacy & consentA cookie banner with real choices: analytics and Google Maps load only after the visitor allows them.
  5. 05Launch & searchLive on amea.ng with HTTPS, plus a sitemap, structured data and social sharing cards.
