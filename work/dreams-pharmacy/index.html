DREAMS PHARMACY

A pharmacy that wanted to look as trustworthy as the medicine it sells. We redrew its initial D so a capsule appears in the negative space.

Client
Dreams Pharmacy & Stores Ltd.
Category
Rebranding
Scope

Services

Brand guidelines ↓

The brief

Dreams Pharmacy & Stores Ltd. needed a modern, professional identity that communicates care and reliability, and works everywhere from a shopping bag to a delivery van.

The approach

The new mark is a bold capital D. Inside it, negative space forms the silhouette of a capsule: the pharmacy’s core business in a single shape. Deep teal for trust, sky blue for health and lime green for wellbeing, set in Cabin.
