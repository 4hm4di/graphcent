AIFF ’26

The Arewa Islamic Finance Forum 2026, convened by IFING Media, brought bankers, regulators, scholars and entrepreneurs together in Kano for five days. Graphcent gave the forum its full visual presence: creative direction, design execution and print consultation, from the first countdown post to the last certificate.

Client
IFING Media Ltd (convener)
Event
18–22 July 2026, Amani Event Center, Kano
Category
Event branding · 2026
Services
Creative Direction, Event Branding, Design Execution, Print Consultation
The creative director’s report ↓Event page ↗

The brief

A forum on the journey of Islamic finance from awareness to adoption and advocacy, with banking, capital markets, pensions, takaful and estate planning on the agenda. It needed to look credible to institutions, welcoming to the public, and consistent everywhere attendees met it: online before the event, at the gate, in the hall and in their hands.

The approach

One system, carried everywhere. A geometric pattern drawn from arches and Arewa motifs, a palette of forest, navy, green, terracotta and sand, and one bold headline style. We set the rules, designed every piece, and worked with the printers so the colours on the banners matched the screens.

Scope

  1. 01Creative directionThe visual system for the forum: pattern, palette, type and how they flex.
  2. 02Social campaignCountdowns, speaker cards, ticket launch, venue reveal, exhibitors and sponsors.
  3. 03Stage & venueStage design, marquee hall banners and the outdoor wayfinding flow.
  4. 04Print kitBadges, agenda and programme, certificates, notebooks and gift bags.
  5. 05Print consultationMaterials, sizes and colour checks with the printers, so everything arrived right.
