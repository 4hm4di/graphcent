PEGASUS
INTERIORS

Pegasus Interiors came to Graphcent with a logo the whole team liked, and one request: something better. We gave them more than a better logo. We built a brand identity: a logo system, an emblem, a palette and stationery, ready to grow into everything the business touches.

Client
Pegasus Interiors, Kano
Category
Brand identity

Services

Read the story: Why a logo is not a brand →

The brief

Pegasus Interiors, an interior design company in Kano, reached out on WhatsApp with their existing logo: a winged horse in front of building outlines. “We all like it,” they said. “It’s just that we want something better.”

The approach

Keep what they loved, the Pegasus and the gold, and rebuild it so it works everywhere. The buildings moved inside the horse: one solid, single-colour symbol that holds up on a favicon, a business card and a billboard. Around it we built the system a brand actually runs on.

Scope

  1. 01Brand symbolThe Pegasus redrawn as one solid shape, with the architecture carried inside the body. Full colour and mono.
  2. 02Logo systemStacked and horizontal lockups, with and without the tagline, on light and dark.
  3. 03EmblemTwo circular seals for stamps, packaging, watermarks and uniforms, on light and dark.
  4. 04ColourPegasus Gold, Charcoal, Cream and Stone: warm, calm and premium, like the spaces they design.
  5. 05StationeryBusiness card, letterhead, invoice and envelope, with the emblem as a watermark.
