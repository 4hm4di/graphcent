AJYAD INVESTMENTS

An investment company working across oil and gas, real estate and agriculture needed one identity for all three. We drew a single drop in three colours, one for each sector.

The brief

Ajyad Investments Limited works in three very different industries: oil and gas, real estate and agriculture. The brand had to feel solid enough for investors and flexible enough to carry each sector, without splitting into three companies.

The approach

The identity is built on a liquid drop divided into three segments. Green for agriculture and growth, yellow for real estate and prosperity, red for the energy of oil and gas. One mark with three meanings, and a palette that lets each sector take the lead when it needs to.

Scope
